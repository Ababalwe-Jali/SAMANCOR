Why Samancor Chrome exists, and the six values behind decisions from the mine face to the smelter floor.

Our purpose
Our purpose

To mine and smelt chrome safely and responsibly, creating lasting value for people, communities and customers.

Zero Harm sits at the centre of how Samancor Chrome operates. Our six values guide the way we work with our employees, suppliers, communities and the steel industry we serve.

Who we are

A progressive and innovative business contributing to the South African economy and mining sector.

Mining, beneficiation and smelting managed as one integrated business.

Our ambition

To be an employer of choice.

Through development, fair reward, a safe workplace and values-driven leadership.

Purpose in practice

Values only matter when they shape the business. These are the structures that carry ours.

Our values

Six Values That Guide Every Decision

Zero Harm
Value / 01

Zero Harm is in our DNA

Doing no harm is at the core of our actions and decisions.

  • A foundational principle, not a standalone campaign
  • Safety, health, environment and quality managed together as SHEQ
  • Environmental documentation publicly available by business unit
Safety firstSHEQ
Integrity
Value / 02

Integrity

The courage to always do the right thing.

  • A Guide to Business Conduct built on honesty and fairness
  • Policies covering legal compliance and stakeholder relationships
  • A whistle-blowing hotline to report suspected fraud
EthicsCompliance
Caring for People
Value / 03

Caring for People

Valuing every individual and treating each other with respect.

  • An employee value proposition built on development and a safe workplace
  • Employment equity and a diverse, representative workforce
  • The Samancor Chrome Foundation, with education as its primary focus
RespectInclusion
Accountability
Value / 04

Accountability

Taking ownership of everything we do.

  • A management accountability framework in our business conduct guide
  • Leadership accountability for employment equity
  • Policies on SHEQ, quality and community, applied across the business
OwnershipLeadership
Collaboration
Value / 05

Collaboration

Working together, valuing employee input and respecting stakeholder relationships.

  • Engagement with unions, NGOs, community agencies and local government
  • Employee input valued in how we work
  • Structured relationships with suppliers and customers
PartnershipStakeholders
Agile Innovation
Value / 06

Agile Innovation

Adapting quickly and innovating to remain sustainable and lead in the industry.

  • Value-chain optimisation under Horizon Two of our strategy
