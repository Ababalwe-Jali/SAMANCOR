Common questions

Governance Questions,
Answered

Quick answers on conduct, reporting and access to information.

What is the Guide to Business Conduct?
Answer

It emphasises integrity, honesty, fairness, high performance and delivery, and provides a framework for management accountability. It supports our policies and standards on SHEQ, quality and community, employment equity, information systems, legal compliance and relationships with governments and stakeholders.

How do I report suspected fraud?
Answer
