Company overview

A South African
Chrome Producer

Samancor Chrome mines chrome ore and smelts it into ferrochrome, supplying the
stainless and special steel industries from operations in North West, Mpumalanga and Limpopo.

Our story

A History Built on Chrome

  1. 1926 – 1937

    SA Manganese (1926) and Amcor (1937) are established, the two companies that later merge to form Samancor.

  2. 1975

    Samancor is established as a listed company on the Johannesburg Stock Exchange.

  3. 1998

    Samancor is delisted after Billiton and Anglo American buy out the minority shareholders.

  4. 2005 – 2006

    The chrome assets are held by the Kermas Group, and the manganese business is unbundled. The business becomes Samancor Chrome Limited.

  5. 2016 – 2017

    Additional ferrochrome smelting and chrome mining assets are acquired.

Our business

From Ore to Alloy in Four Steps

View our operations
Step / 01

Mine the Ore

Chrome ore is extracted from the Bushveld Igneous Complex through underground and opencast mining.

Step / 02

Beneficiate

Beneficiation plants prepare and concentrate the ore, and tailings re-treatment recovers further value.

Step / 03

Smelt into Ferrochrome

Furnaces convert chrome-bearing feed into ferrochrome, principally high-carbon ferrochrome known as charge chrome.

Step / 04

Supply the Steel Industry

Chromium gives stainless steel its corrosion resistance. We supply ferrochrome and chrome ore to steel customers.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Samancor Chrome do?

We mine chrome ore, beneficiate it and smelt it into ferrochrome, mainly charge chrome, for stainless and special steel producers. We also sell chrome ore.

Where do you operate?

Our mining and smelting operations are in North West, Mpumalanga and Limpopo, supported by a corporate office in Sandton, Gauteng.

What products do you sell?

High-carbon ferrochrome (charge chrome) and chrome ore. See our products page for more.

How do I make a sales enquiry?

Sales enquiries are handled by email rather than by telephone. Include your company description, location, required product and chemical specification, tonnage and estimated delivery date. A marketer will respond within five working days. Details are on our sales information page.

Who owns Samancor Chrome?

Samancor Chrome Holdings (Pty) Limited is the sole shareholder of Samancor Chrome Limited.

Where can I find environmental documents?

Environmental documentation for all business units is available through our SHEQ information page.
