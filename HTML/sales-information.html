Questions

Before You
Make an Enquiry

How do I place an order or request product information?

Send an email to our sales team with the details listed above. Orders and product information requests are handled by email.

Can I place an order by telephone?

No. Orders and product information requests are not handled by telephone.

How long will it take to receive a response?

A marketer will respond within five working days.

What products can I enquire about?

Charge chrome (high-carbon ferrochrome), chrome ore, and processed products including metallurgical and chemical concentrates and some foundry grades.

How do I tell you which specification I need?

Include the product and chemical specification you require in your enquiry, together with tonnage and estimated delivery date.
