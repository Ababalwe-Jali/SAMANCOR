Three Horizons

Three Horizons, One Direction

The three-horizon approach was initially tabled in 2001 and continues to frame our priorities.

Horizon One

Safe production

  • A manufacturing mindset
  • Safe delivery of production
  • A stable operating environment
Horizon Two

Value-chain optimisation

  • Rationalising mining operations
  • Deploying capital
  • Removing bottlenecks to improve returns
Horizon Three

Future positioning

  • Anticipating industry conditions
  • Pursuing emerging opportunities
  • Repositioning the business
