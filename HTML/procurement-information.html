Our approach

Procurement Through
Group Procurement Services

Group Procurement Services manages procurement at Samancor Chrome. New suppliers apply to join our e-procurement platform, and existing suppliers manage purchase orders through the Coupa Supplier Portal.

Use the routes on this page to find the right next step for your business.
