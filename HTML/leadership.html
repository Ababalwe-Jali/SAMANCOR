Our leadership

Samancor Chrome is led by an executive team accountable for safe production, performance across the value chain and responsible business conduct.

Desmond McManusChief Executive Officer→

Desmond McManus

Chief Executive Officer

Desmond holds a Bachelor in Engineering (Extractive Metallurgy), as well as an MBL (Masters in Business Leadership) Degree from UNISA. His past working experience includes being a Production Superintendent as Assmang Manganese's Cato Ridge Works, before joining Samancor Chrome as a Production Superintendent at Tubatse Ferrochrome in 2002.

In 2005, he was promoted to the position of Operations Manager of Tubatse Ferrochrome. In 2008, he was promoted to General Manager of Tubatse Ferrochrome. Desmond was promoted to COO of Samancor Chrome on 1 January 2013. He has been in his current position since 1 July 2019.

Leader / 02

George Kleynhans

Chief Operating Officer: Mining

George has extensive mining industry experience and holds a B.Tech in Mining Engineering, as well as Mine Overseer and Mine Manager Certificates. He began his career at Anglo Platinum before progressing through senior management roles at Aquarius Platinum, Impala Platinum and Sibanye-Stillwater.

He joined Sibanye-Stillwater’s Kwezi Operations in 2016 as Mine Manager, later serving as Acting Vice-President and Vice-President and Head of Operations at Mooinooi Operations. He joined Samancor Chrome and has held his current position since July 2022.
