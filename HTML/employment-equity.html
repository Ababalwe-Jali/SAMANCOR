Our commitment

Opportunity That Is
Open to Everyone

Employment equity means equal opportunity and the elimination of unfair discrimination. Samancor Chrome is committed to a diverse and representative workforce.

We comply with the Mining Charter and the Employment Equity Act, and our leaders are accountable for progress.

Our principles

What Guides
Our Approach

Six principles from our employment equity commitments.

Equal opportunity

Everyone has a genuine chance to be hired, to grow and to progress.

No unfair discrimination

We work to eliminate unfair discrimination.

Leadership accountability

Leaders are accountable for employment equity.

Legal compliance

We comply with the Mining Charter and the Employment Equity Act.

A representative workforce

We aim for a workforce that is diverse and representative.

Removing barriers

We work to remove barriers to employment.
