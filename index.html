About us

An integrated South African chrome producer

From mine to furnace, Samancor Chrome produces chrome ore and ferrochrome for the steel industry.

About Us

We mine chrome ore, beneficiate it and smelt it into ferrochrome at sites across North West, Mpumalanga and Limpopo.

Explore our business
Company profile

Samancor Chrome extracts chrome ore from the Bushveld Igneous Complex and processes it into ferrochrome, an essential input to stainless steel. Mining, beneficiation and smelting are managed as one integrated business, supported by a corporate office in Sandton.

How we work

One Integrated Value Chain

  1. Stage 1

    Resource

    Chrome ore deposits in the Bushveld Igneous Complex.

  2. Stage 2

    Mining

    Underground and opencast extraction.

  3. Stage 3

    Beneficiation

    Ore preparation, concentration and tailings re-treatment.

  4. Stage 4

    Smelting

    Furnaces convert feed material into ferrochrome.

  5. Stage 5

    Products

    Charge chrome (high-carbon ferrochrome) and chrome ore.

  6. Stage 6

    Customers

    Stainless and special steel producers.
